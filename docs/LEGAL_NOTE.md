# Legal note

**This is not legal advice.**

Fee-share tokens may be treated as securities or as a regulated financial product in some
jurisdictions. A token whose value derives from a continuing stream of trading fees can look like a
profit-sharing instrument to a regulator, even when it is issued permissionlessly.

Before doing anything beyond a hackathon demonstration:

- Get counsel in the jurisdictions you intend to serve.
- Consider geo-restricting the interface.
- Do not market returns. The valuation engine produces a range, never a promise, and every number in
  the UI is a model output.
- Keep the "unaudited" disclosure visible.
